          New series

          Your list of series is kept at a drop of yours, signed by you and encrypted for you alone, so that you find it on any node you sign in on. A series itself is held by the network, encrypted, and can be read only by whoever is given its id.

            Add episodes from files

            The folders this node offers. Choose a video, and it is imported into the network, and added to this series, in its season. Its season and episode come from its file's name, as S01E02 or 1x02, or from a number in its name, in a folder named for its season, such as Season 1, or Specials. A file of more than one episode is named for each, as S01E01E02, or for the first and last, as S01E01-02. Or add every video in the folder shown, and in the folders within it.

              Each video found, numbered as its file's name gives. Correct any, number those it does not, and leave out any you choose. An episode is a number, such as 2, or more than one, such as 1, 2, for a file holding each. Season 0 is the specials. Those whose numbers this series holds already are left out.

                Its file's name gives no season and episode, as S01E02 or 1x02 would, and the folder it is in is named for no season.

                Season 0 is the specials.

                Looking series up at TMDB

                A new series is looked up at TMDB, The Movie Database, by the name given, and you choose which of the series of that name it is. Its name, the year it first aired, its rating, cast, description, and a backdrop are taken from there. So is what is known of each episode added from a file, and of its season, by its numbers: its title, air date, description, duration, and a still. Each can be edited after. Only those names and numbers, and the key, are sent to TMDB. TMDB gives a key to anyone with an account at themoviedb.org, in its settings, under API. This is the movie application's key too, so a key given in either serves both. It is kept at a drop of yours, signed by you and encrypted for you alone, as your series are. Left empty, nothing is looked up, here or in the movie application.

                This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

                Add an episode by its id

                The id an episode was shared by. It is added to this series, in its season, by its numbers.

                Share this series

                Whoever is given this id can play the whole series as it is now, and import it on their own node. No one else can read it.

                Part of it

                Choose the seasons to share, each with every episode it holds, and an id is made for them alone. Imported into a series of the same name, they are added to it, so that each new season can be shared as it comes.

                  Keep seasons offline

                  The seasons chosen, each with every episode it holds, are kept on this node, in a series of their own, and play here even once it is offline, until the day chosen. Signing in still works offline too. What you watch of them there is not kept.

                    Whoever is given this id can play this episode, and add it to a series of their own, in its season.

                    Import a series

                    The id a series, or seasons of one, was shared by. Its seasons are added to the series of its name you keep, or else make a new series of yours.

                      Keys

                      Anywhere on the page but in a field, and while no other dialog is open.

                      ← → ↑ ↓
                      Move through the episodes, the seasons, or the series, as they are laid out, from the one with focus.
                      Home End
                      To the first, or the last.
                      Enter
                      Choose the episode or the season, or open the series.
                      Space G
                      Play or pause the episode chosen. Space presses a button that has focus instead.
                      F
                      Full screen, or back.
                      S X
                      Stop, and leave the episode: out of full screen, and back to it in its season.
                      N P
                      The next episode, or the one before, on into the next season or back into the one before, playing on if one was playing.
                      [ ]
                      The season before, or the next, in the series.
                      C
                      Choose another series.
                      `
                      Filter the episodes of every season, as the magnifying glass does, to those with every whole word typed. Enter keeps the filter, and Escape empties it. It starts empty each time, so ` then Enter shows the seasons again.
