      Details

      Import photos

      The folders this node offers. Choose photos in any of them with their boxes, and import those chosen, or import every photo in the folder shown, and in the folders within it. Each import makes a new album, named for when it is made, and imports each photo encrypted, so that only whoever is given the album can see it. A photo of a kind not every browser shows, as HEIC and raw files, is viewed as a JPEG this browser makes of it, or finds within it, or else kept to be downloaded. Videos are not imported yet.

        Each photo found, by its path. Leave out any you choose. Those left are imported one after another into a new album, named Import, then the date and time.

          New album

          Your list of albums is kept at a drop of yours, signed by you and encrypted for you alone, so that you find it on any node you sign in on. An album itself is held by the network, encrypted, and can be read only by whoever is given its id.

          Rename the album

          It is taken out of your albums, with every photo it holds. Photos it shares with another album stay in that one.

          Keys
