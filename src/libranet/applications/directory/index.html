If your identity is compromised

People are listed here by their id, the content id of their public key, once they open this page signed in, or are added from a link, such as the one the administration page gives for a user it creates. The list is kept at the drop user directory, and opening this page merges what each copy found there lists. What each person says of themselves is kept at a drop of their own, user: and their id, signed by them. Each version names their fallback, an identity made with theirs that takes over should theirs be compromised, and the version it replaces. The page reads each person's versions back to the first, and an identity whose versions name another fallback, or none, or whose fallback says so, is compromised. A fallback that says so takes over, with a fallback of its own.
