If your identity is compromised

People are listed here by their id, the content id of their public key, once they open this page signed in, or are added from a link, such as the one the administration page gives for a user it creates. The list is kept at the drop user directory, and opening this page merges what each copy found there lists. What each person says of themselves is kept at a drop of their own, user: and their id, signed by them. Each version names their fallback, an identity made with theirs that takes over should theirs be compromised, and the version it replaces. The page reads each person's versions back to the first, and an identity whose versions name another fallback, or none, or whose fallback says so, is compromised. A fallback that says so takes over, with a fallback of its own. Whom each person trusts and distrusts is their trust list, kept by the node at the drop trust: and their id, signed by them, and public, distrust included. Each person's mark is from the scores the node builds from your list, the lists of those you trust, and so on onward: 🟢 above 0, trusted; 🟡 0, neither; 🔴 below 0, distrusted; and ⚪ where there are no scores to show. Beside it is their Karma, their balance as the Karma chain this node follows states it, linked to the Karma application. A link to /directory/?id= and a person's id shows them alone, with whom their trust list names.
