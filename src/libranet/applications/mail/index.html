Add someone

Each message is a copy for each person it is sent to, signed by its sender and encrypted for that person alone, at the drop mail: and their id, followed by the hour it was sent in UTC, such as 2026/10/12/09. A person reads a period by searching each hour of it. The sender keeps a copy of their own at mail sent:, their id, and the hour. Your address book, and what you have read and hidden, are kept the same way, encrypted for you, at mail address book: and your id, and at mail state:, your id, and each month of mail. Mail is read and sent only in a browser on the node's own machine, signed in. Each person's mark is their trust, by the scores the node builds from your trust list, the lists of those you trust, and so on onward: 🟢 above 0, trusted; 🟡 0, neither; 🔴 below 0, distrusted; and ⚪ where there are no scores to show. Each person's name links to their page in the user directory.
