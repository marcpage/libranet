                  Keys

                  Anywhere on the page but in a field, and while no other dialog is open.

                  ← → ↑ ↓
                  Move through the movies, or the collections, as they are laid out, from the one with focus.
                  Home End
                  To the first, or the last.
                  Enter
                  Choose the movie, or open the collection.
                  Space G
                  Play or pause the movie chosen. Space presses a button that has focus instead.
                  F
                  Full screen, or back.
                  S X
                  Stop, and leave the movie: out of full screen, and back to it in the collection.
                  N P
                  The next movie, or the one before, playing on if one was playing.
                  C
                  Choose another collection.
                  `
                  Filter the movies listed, as the magnifying glass does, to those with every whole word typed. Enter keeps the filter, and Escape empties it. It starts empty each time, so ` then Enter shows every movie again.
                  ? /
                  These keys.
