    #TitleArtist AlbumTime

      Add songs from files

      The folders this node offers. Choose a song, and it is imported into the network, and added to your library. Its title, artist, album, and the rest come from its tags, once it is imported. What they do not give comes from its path: its title from its file's name, less a track number it starts with, its album from the folder it is in, and its artist from the folder above that, as a library laid out as Artist/Album/01 Title.mp3 gives. Its cover is the picture its tags hold, or else one beside it named cover, folder, front, or album. Each can be edited after. Or add every song in the folder shown, and in the folders within it.

        Each song found. Leave out any you choose. Those your library holds already are left out. Your library is kept at a drop of yours, signed by you and encrypted for you alone, so that you find it on any node you sign in on.

          What is edited is kept in your library. The file's own tags are not changed.

          Keys

          Anywhere on the page but in a field, and while no other dialog is open.

          ↑ ↓ ← →
          Move through the songs listed, from the one with focus.
          Home End
          To the first, or the last.
          Enter
          Play the song with focus, and then the songs after it in the list, or pause it if it is playing.
          Space G
          Play or pause the song chosen. Space presses a button that has focus instead.
          N P
          Play the next song, or the one before.
          S
          Shuffle on, or off.
          `
          Filter the songs, as the magnifying glass does, to those with every whole word typed in any of their details. Enter keeps the filter, and Escape empties it. It starts empty each time, so ` then Enter shows every song the artist and album chosen do.
          ? /
          These keys.
