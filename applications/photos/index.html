      Import photos

      The folders this node offers. Choose photos in any of them with their boxes, and import those chosen, or import every photo in the folder shown, and in the folders within it. Each import makes a new album, named for when it is made, and imports each photo encrypted, so that only whoever is given the album can see it. A photo of a kind not every browser shows, as HEIC and raw files, is viewed as a JPEG this browser makes of it, or finds within it, or else kept to be downloaded. A video is imported as a photo is, and played as a browser plays it. A photo beside a video of its name, five seconds long at most, is a Live Photo, which keeps the video as its motion.

        Each photo found, by its path. Leave out any you choose. Those left are imported one after another into a new album, named Import, then the date and time.

          New album

          Your list of albums is kept at a drop of yours, signed by you and encrypted for you alone, so that you find it on any node you sign in on. An album itself is held by the network, encrypted, and can be read only by whoever is given its id.

          Rename the album

          It is taken out of your albums, with every photo it holds. Photos it shares with another album stay in that one.

            Each is taken out of this album, with what is known of it. A copy of it in another album stays in that one.

            Keys

            Anywhere on the page but in a field, and while no other dialog is open.

            ← → ↑ ↓
            Move through the albums, or the photos of the album open, as they are laid out, from the one with focus.
            Home End
            To the first album or photo, or the last.
            Page Up Page Down
            Move through the photos of the album open by a screen.
            Enter
            Open the album with focus, or show the photo with focus, or, while choosing photos, choose it or leave it out.
            Esc Backspace
            Stop choosing photos, or back to the albums.
            N
            Make a new album.
            S
            Choose photos of the album open, to copy, move, or remove them, or stop choosing. A click with Shift chooses every photo from the last chosen, and a day's heading every photo of that day.
            ? /
            These keys.

            With a photo shown

            ← →
            The photo before, or after, in the album's order.
            I
            Its details: when it was taken, the camera, and where, or hide them.
            Z
            Zoom to the photo's own pixels, or fit it to the window again.
            F
            Full screen, or leave it.
            D
            Download its original.
            Esc
            Back to the album, at the photo shown.
